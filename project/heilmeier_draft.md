I am building a system that helps a device find its exact location inside a building where GPS cannot reach by looking at wireless signal patterns as if they
were digital pictures.

Currently, we use simple math to measure distances from signals, but this fails because signals bounce off walls and furniture, creating messy data that 
leads to large errors in location.

My approach uses a smart neural network to recognize these complex patterns instead of just measuring them. It will be successful because it can find a 
location even with messy signals and is designed to be small and efficient enough to run on low-power microchips.
